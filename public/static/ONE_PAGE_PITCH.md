## 🏆 What We Have That GBVIMS+ Will NEVER Have

### 1. 🚨 **Emergency SOS Button**
- Survivor presses button → **Auto-calls Police (019)** with GPS location
- Sends SMS to trusted contacts: "I need help. I'm at [location]"
- **Saves lives in domestic violence emergencies**
- GBVIMS+ has NOTHING like this

### 2. 🎙️ **AI Voice Reporting**
- Survivor **speaks** their report (no writing needed)
- System **asks questions**, captures verbal answers
- **70% of Sierra Leone women are illiterate** — this empowers them
- GBVIMS+ requires writing forms

### 3. 🙋‍♀️ **Survivor Self-Portal**
- Survivors **track their own cases** 24/7
- See services provided, upcoming appointments
- Access emergency hotlines anytime
- GBVIMS+ is provider-only (survivors never see it)
